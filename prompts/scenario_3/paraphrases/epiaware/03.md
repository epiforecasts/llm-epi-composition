Joint inference of a single time-varying R_t from three surveillance streams

Goal
- Infer one shared, time-varying effective reproduction number R_t that is coherent with all three observation series: cases, hospitalisations, and deaths.
- The three streams are independent measurements of the same epidemic.

Data scope and location
- Observation window: 150 consecutive days, from 2023-01-01 through 2023-05-30.
- Input files and columns:
  - data/cases.csv — columns: date, cases
  - data/hospitalisations.csv — columns: date, hospitalisations
  - data/deaths.csv — columns: date, deaths

Epidemiological quantities (fixed)
- Treat these as known from external studies; do not propagate uncertainty.
- Generation interval: Gamma distribution with mean 5.5 days and standard deviation 2 days.
- Reporting delay from infection to observation (log-normal):
  - Cases: mean 5 days, standard deviation 2 days
  - Hospitalisations: mean 10 days, standard deviation 3 days
  - Deaths: mean 20 days, standard deviation 5 days

Data structure and modeling considerations
- All three series reflect the same underlying epidemic and must share a single R_t trajectory.
- Each stream has its own reporting delay (as above) and its own time-varying ascertainment. These ascertainment trajectories differ by stream and are unknown.
- Counts exhibit a weekly (day-of-week) reporting pattern.
- Observation noise is overdispersed compared with a homogeneous Poisson process.

Modeling task
- Produce daily point estimates of the unified R_t over the full period using the renewal equation for infections.
- Explicitly incorporate:
  - Stream-specific delays,
  - Stream-specific, time-varying ascertainment,
  - Day-of-week effects,
  - Overdispersed observation noise.

Implementation requirements
- Use the EpiAware.jl Julia package.
- Build the model using EpiAware’s primitives. The API reference is available in epiaware_docs.md for types and functions.

Output
- Write final R_t estimates to outputs/rt_estimates.csv with columns:
  - date (YYYY-MM-DD)
  - Rt_median (point estimate)
  - Rt_lower (5th-percentile credible/confidence bound, optional but encouraged)
  - Rt_upper (95th-percentile credible/confidence bound, optional but encouraged)

Quality and robustness
- If your code encounters errors, diagnose and resolve them.
- If the results appear implausible, revise the model and re-run.
