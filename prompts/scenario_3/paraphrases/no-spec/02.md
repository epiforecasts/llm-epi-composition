Estimate a single time-varying effective reproduction number, Rt, that aligns with three independent observation streams (cases, hospitalisations, deaths) from the same epidemic.

Scope and core requirement
- Produce daily point estimates of one shared Rt trajectory consistent with all three data streams.
- Use the renewal equation for the infection process.
- Explicitly model stream-specific reporting delays, time-varying ascertainment for each stream, a day-of-week reporting pattern, and observation overdispersion.

Observation window and inputs
- Observation period: 150 days spanning 2023-01-01 to 2023-05-30.
- Files and columns:
  - data/cases.csv with columns date, cases.
  - data/hospitalisations.csv with columns date, hospitalisations.
  - data/deaths.csv with columns date, deaths.

Fixed epidemiological quantities (no uncertainty propagation)
- Generation interval: Gamma distribution with mean 5.5 days and standard deviation 2 days.
- Infection-to-observation reporting delays, each log-normal:
  - Cases: mean 5 days, standard deviation 2 days.
  - Hospitalisations: mean 10 days, standard deviation 3 days.
  - Deaths: mean 20 days, standard deviation 5 days.

Structural assumptions about the data
- The three series are independent observations of the same underlying epidemic and share a single Rt trajectory.
- Each stream has its own reporting delay (as above) and its own time-varying ascertainment (the proportion of infections that are ultimately reported in that stream). These ascertainment trajectories are stream-specific and unknown.
- The counts exhibit a weekly reporting cycle.
- Observation noise is overdispersed relative to a homogeneous Poisson process.

Output specification
- Write the final Rt estimates to outputs/rt_estimates.csv with columns:
  - date (YYYY-MM-DD)
  - Rt_median (point estimate)
  - Rt_lower (5th-percentile credible/confidence bound; optional but encouraged)
  - Rt_upper (95th-percentile credible/confidence bound; optional but encouraged)

Implementation notes
- You may use any language or framework.
- If the code encounters errors, diagnose and correct them. If the inferred results appear implausible, revise the model and run it again.
